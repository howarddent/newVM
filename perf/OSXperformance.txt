\b Notes\b0\par
\f1\fs16 - MatMult backend: PUREPASCAL = plain triple loop; Accelerate = cblas_dgemm.\par
- LinearSolve/Invert backend: PUREPASCAL = PurePascalLU/PurePascalLUSolve; Accelerate = dgetrf_/dgetri_/dgetrs_ (Apple vecLib, row-major/column-major adapter).\par
- Residuals for both variants: solve residual (||A*x - b||) and invert residual (||A*Ainv - I||) both landed in the 1e-11 to 1e-15 range - both variants compute correct results; the difference is purely speed.\par
- Machine: Darwin/aarch64 (Apple Silicon), no MKL/IPP/OpenBLAS installed - Accelerate is the only non-PUREPASCAL backend available.\par
- Produced by newVMbench.lpr (repo root), built twice against two different newVMConfig.inc contents (real Accelerate-detected config, and a forced-PUREPASCAL config) - see that file's header comment for exact build/run steps.\par
