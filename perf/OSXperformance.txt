- LinearSolve/Invert (real double, real single) backend: PUREPASCAL = PurePascalLU?/PurePascalLU?Solve; Accelerate = ?getrf_/?getri_/?getrs_ (Apple vecLib, row-major/column-major adapter).\par
- InvertZ/InvertC backend: PUREPASCAL = PurePascalLUZ|C/PurePascalLUSolveZ|C; Accelerate = zgetrf_/zgetri_ or cgetrf_/cgetri_.\par
- LinearSolveZ and LinearSolveC have NO Accelerate path at all, in either build: Apple's zgetrs_/cgetrs_ crash with an access violation whenever a factored pivot has an exactly-zero imaginary part (confirmed via a standalone reproduction independent of this codebase - see cblas.pas's comments next to accel_zgetrf_/accel_zgetri_ and accel_cgetrf_/accel_cgetri_, and newVMComplex.pas's/newVMComplexSingle.pas's LinearSolveZ/LinearSolveC). Both always run their PurePascalLU?/PurePascalLU?Solve path, so their two timings above are expected to be near-identical - that is not a bug in this benchmark.\par
- LinearSolveS at N=100 measured slower under Accelerate (2.63ms) than PUREPASCAL (0.56ms) in one run of this benchmark, while N=1000 shows Accelerate ~252x faster as expected - most likely small-N call/setup overhead dominating at that size (a later re-run of the same benchmark did not reproduce this at N=100), but not fully isolated; flagged here rather than smoothed over.\par
- Residuals: solve residual (||A*x - b||) and invert residual (||A*Ainv - I|| or its complex-magnitude equivalent) landed in the 1e-11 to 1e-15 range for double/complex double, and roughly 1e-6 to 1e-2 for single/complex single (expected float32 precision) - all eight combinations (double/single/complex double/complex single x both builds) compute correct results; the differences above are purely speed.\par
